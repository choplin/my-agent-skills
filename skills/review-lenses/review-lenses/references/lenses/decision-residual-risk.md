# `decision.residual-risk`

## Objective

Find material uncertainty hidden by the completion summary and make any required
human risk acceptance explicit.

## Required inputs

- skipped or inconclusive checks
- unresolved, rejected, or unremediated findings
- one-way autonomous decisions and stated residual risks

## Checks

- Reconcile unresolved findings and coverage gaps.
- Test whether stated residual risks describe consequence and exposure.
- Find assumptions presented as verified facts.
- Identify decisions that exceeded delegated authority.

## Non-goals

- Do not turn immaterial uncertainty into a blocking risk.
- Do not duplicate an actionable defect already recorded as a finding.

## Severity guidance

Put uncertainty under residual risks unless evidence establishes a defect. Use a
finding only when hidden or misrepresented risk violates a binding constraint or
delegated authority.
