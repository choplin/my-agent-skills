# Book or chapter plot

Adapt this template to nonfiction, narrative, or a mixed work. It is a set of
prompts, not a required beat sheet.

## Promise of the whole

What lasting question, experience, or transformation does the book promise?
What makes this treatment distinct? What must the reader bring with them?

## Arc

Describe the book's movement in prose, cards, or a diagram. For each part or
chapter, note the work it performs for the whole and the change it leaves in the
reader. Track dependencies and deliberate echoes across chapter boundaries.

## Nonfiction prompts

- What is the controlling argument or explanatory journey?
- Which claims are load-bearing, and what grounds them?
- Where do examples create understanding rather than ornament?
- Which concepts deserve development, and which should remain ordinary prose?
- Where should objections, alternatives, or limitations enter?

## Narrative prompts

- What desire, conflict, causality, or question carries the movement?
- What changes in knowledge, stakes, relation, or agency?
- Why is each scene or episode present, and why here?
- Whose viewpoint governs what can be revealed?

## Chapter or section cards

Use any fields that help: provisional title, entry pressure, central movement,
evidence or scene material, turn, exit state, bridge, unresolved problem.

## Rhythm and proportion

Mark where the work compresses, expands, pauses, accelerates, recalls, or lands.
Check that space follows importance rather than source volume.

## Revision note

What changed in this revision, and why?
